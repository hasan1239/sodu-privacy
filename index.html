Sodu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026

Sodu is a daily Sudoku app. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. Sodu is built to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting trackers.

What we collect

When you finish a daily puzzle (by solving it, using all five strikes, or giving up), the app sends a single record to our stats service so we can show you how your result compares with other players. That record contains:

  • An anonymous device identifier — a random ID generated on your device when you install the app. It is not your name, email, phone number, Google account, advertising ID, or any hardware identifier, and it cannot be used to identify you.
  • The puzzle identifier, date, and difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard).
  • The outcome — whether you won or lost.
  • Your completion time and whether the solve included any mistakes.
  • The time the result was submitted.

This is the only data that leaves your device. Puzzles played in Unlimited Puzzles mode are never submitted. Your personal stats, settings, and progress are stored only on your device.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or account — Sodu has no sign-in.
  • No advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI, or other persistent device identifiers.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files, or other personal content.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Where your data is stored

Submitted results are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon in Amazon Web Services' eu-west-2 (London) region. The service is fronted by Cloudflare Workers, which processes requests at the network edge; your data is never stored outside the eu-west-2 region. Cloudflare may briefly handle standard request metadata (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the service, but the app does not log, store, or correlate this. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Neon and Cloudflare act as data processors on our behalf; we do not sell or share your data with any third party for their own purposes.

Payments

Sodu offers one optional one-time purchase, "Unlimited Puzzles". Payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details. Whether you own the purchase is confirmed with Google Play and stored only on your device. Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Submitted puzzle results are kept so comparative statistics stay accurate over time. We do not have a fixed deletion schedule — instead, you can delete your data at any time, and we honour that immediately (see below).

Your choices and rights

  • View your identifier: your anonymous device ID is shown in the app's Settings.
  • Delete your data: tap "Delete all my stats" in Settings. This erases all data stored on your device and permanently deletes every result associated with your device ID from our server. Afterwards your device is treated as a fresh install.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data. To also remove your submitted results from the server, use "Delete all my stats" before uninstalling.

If you are in the UK or EU, you have rights under the UK GDPR / GDPR including access, rectification, and erasure. Because Sodu holds no information that identifies you personally, the in-app deletion tool is the most direct way to exercise these rights. You can also contact us using the details below.

Children

Sodu is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
